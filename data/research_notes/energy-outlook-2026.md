# Energy sector outlook 2026

Desk view: neutral. Oil prices are expected to stay in a wide range, and the desk's base
case assumes a 25% fall in energy share prices as the severe-but-plausible stress scenario.

- Integrated oil companies: stable cash flows, high sensitivity to oil prices.
- Solar and other renewables: faster growth, more volatile share prices.
- Suggested limit: energy should stay below 20% of a moderate client's portfolio.
